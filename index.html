THE WIL 5.0 CONVERSATION

An equal voice.
A lasting impact.

Leadership is about shaping decisions, communicating your value and creating opportunities for others. It also needs workplaces where women's contributions are heard, recognised and acted upon.

WIL 5.0 brings together women leaders, industry professionals, entrepreneurs, academicians, programme alumni and emerging professionals to connect ambition with meaningful action.
